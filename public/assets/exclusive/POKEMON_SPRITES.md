# Pokémon sprite source

The Pokémon prototype assets in the numeric files below were downloaded from the
[PokeAPI sprites repository](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites), using the
`generation-v/black-white` animated front/back GIF and static PNG collections.
The numeric filenames are National Pokédex IDs and are selected through
`src/data/pokemonMapping.js`.

These assets depict Pokémon and may be subject to third-party intellectual-property
rights. Their inclusion on the `poke` branch is for a non-commercial fangame prototype
and does not imply that they are safe to redistribute or merge into the production
`main` branch.

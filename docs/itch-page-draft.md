## Description

Pixel Monster is a browser-based virtual pet RPG about raising a small digital creature into a battle-ready companion.

Feed it, care for it, train it, and watch its personality and history shape how it evolves. Each monster can grow through branching evolution paths, learn new moves, collect items, and take part in wild battles, trainer battles, tournaments, and online player-versus-player matches.

Features:

- Raise a pixel monster through hunger, mood, sleep, bond, training, and daily care.
- Discover branching evolutions driven by type affinity, personality, bond, battle history, and rebirth events.
- Battle wild monsters, trainers, tournament opponents, and other players with turn-based skills and status effects.
- Collect monsters, items, move upgrades, diary entries, and daily letters from your companion.
- Switch between Traditional Chinese and English in the game settings.
- Play directly in the browser with no installation required.

Controls:

- Use the on-screen A, B, and C buttons to navigate menus and confirm actions.
- Keyboard controls may also work depending on your browser, but the on-screen buttons are the primary input.

Notes:

- Cloud save and online features require browser network access.
- If audio does not start immediately, click or tap inside the game first.
